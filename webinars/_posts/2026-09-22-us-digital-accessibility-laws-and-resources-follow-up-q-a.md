### Description

During the July webinar, "US Digital Accessibility: Laws, Resources, and Real-World Insights," attendees submitted more questions than we had time to address. To continue the conversation, our presenters are returning for a follow-up session focused on answering the questions about Section 508 and ADA Title 2 Web and Mobile Applications Accessibility.

Real-time captioning and sign language interpretation will be provided.

Registrants will be able to submit questions in advance during the registration process.
